# Privacy Policy

**Change Audio Device for Command Palette**

*Last updated: June 22, 2026*

## Overview

Change Audio Device is an open-source extension for Microsoft Command Palette that allows users to quickly change their active audio playback and recording devices. Your privacy is important to us, and this extension is designed with that in mind.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** This extension does not gather, store, or transmit any personal information, usage analytics, telemetry, or tracking data of any kind. 

## Data Storage

All data processed by this extension (such as the list of active audio devices) is processed entirely in memory on your local machine. No data is saved to disk or sent to any remote server.

## Data Sharing

**We do not share any data.** Since no data is collected or stored, there is nothing to share with third parties.

## Third-Party Services

This extension operates completely locally and does not interact with any third-party APIs, web services, or external websites.

## Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to open an issue in the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/brunnobb/ChangeAudioDeviceCommandPalleteExtension).
